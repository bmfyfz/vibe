# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone speaks for the project.

In short:

- Be respectful. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Assume the other person meant well, and say plainly when something didn't land that way.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no sexualised language or imagery, and nobody's private details posted without their say.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here crosses that line, tell the maintainers privately. Open a report through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, which reaches only the maintainers, and say it is a conduct report. Say what happened, where, and who was involved. You will get an answer, and what you report is kept between you and the maintainers.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may ban someone, for a time or for good, when it is warranted. The enforcement guidelines are the Contributor Covenant's own.
